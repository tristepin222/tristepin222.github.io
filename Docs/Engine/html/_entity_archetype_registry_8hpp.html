Register an entity archetype that appears in hierarchy creation menus.

Parameters
MenuPathMenu path, e.g. "3D Objects/Cube" or "UI/Canvas".
TypeStringString passed to Scene::createEntityOfType or createPrimitiveEntity.
IsPrimitiveTrue for geometry primitives (Cube/Triangle/Quad), false otherwise.

Example: REGISTER_ENTITY_ARCHETYPE("3D Objects/Cube", "Cube", true); REGISTER_ENTITY_ARCHETYPE("Rendering & Lights/Camera", "Camera", false);
